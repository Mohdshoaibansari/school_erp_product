Flow 10 — Ownership Transfer Cross-Cutting

← Back to all flows
This flow: Transfer an institution from Client A to Client B with Platform Owner approval (D12). Requires two clients both with active institutions.
Prerequisites: Run Flow 9 (creates Sunrise client) + Flow 1+2 (creates Greenwood client). Stored vars: PLATFORM_TOKEN, INST_ID (Greenwood), BOOT_INST_ID (Sunrise), CLIENT_ID (Greenwood), BOOT_CLIENT_ID (Sunrise).

Steps

Scenario: Transfer Greenwood's institution to Sunrise (or vice versa). Let's transfer Greenwood → Sunrise.
1Request Ownership Transfer
Initiated by Platform Owner. Records an Approval row in pending state.
2Approve Transfer
Executes the transfer in a single DB transaction (D12). Institution's client_id moves A → B.
3Verify Institution is now under Sunrise
The institution's client_id should now match BOOT_CLIENT_ID.
4Sunrise Director lists institutions — should now include transferred one
Should show both Sunrise Main Campus AND the transferred Greenwood institution.
✅ Flow 10 complete. Ownership transfer executed with proper approval flow.
⚠ C-11 audit immutability: Per D12, audit events recorded before the transfer keep their original ClientId — historical truth is preserved.
